2 Add it to your browser

Already using the QA Tool from a zip? Open it first and press ⋮ → Settings → Your data → Back up my data. Then carry on below. The copy from OneDrive takes the place of the old one, and your reviews stay. Afterwards you can delete the old unzipped folder.
  1. Open a new tab and go to the extensions page:
    Paste it into the address bar and press Enter.
  2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked.
  4. Choose the QA Tool folder from step 1 (under OneDrive - SOTI, the one with the shortcut arrow) and click Select Folder. Pick the folder itself, not a file inside it.
  5. The QA Tool appears in the list. Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar and click the pin beside QA Tool, so its teal QA icon stays on your toolbar.

3 Open it

  1. Click the teal QA icon on your toolbar. The QA Tool opens at the side.
  2. Accept the terms of use. When the Connect Copilot box appears, click Grant access to Copilot and allow it.
  3. In ⋮ → Settings, at the bottom, you will see ✓ Verified original: you have the genuine copy.
Updates are automatic. When a new version is published, OneDrive brings it to your laptop and the QA Tool restarts itself onto it. If you are using it at that moment, a bar says “Version … is ready — Restart now”. Your reviews, chats and settings are kept.
Using Microsoft Edge? Follow the same steps. On Edge, Developer mode is at the bottom of the left-hand menu, and Load unpacked appears at the top of the page once it is on.
Leave Developer mode on. Turning it off switches the QA Tool off. Your browser may mention “developer mode extensions”. That is normal for tools installed this way.

Please do not change, move or delete anything inside the QA Tool folder. It is kept in step with the shared copy for everyone, and a changed file marks your copy as unofficial.
