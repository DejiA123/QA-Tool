The standards this tool measures against

These are the numbers every measured finding is compared with. They are settings rather than constants because a threshold nobody can change is one people work around — if the first response target moves, change it here rather than waiting for a new build.

The team

The agents the coaching sheet has a row for, one per line. A name here is matched against the case owner Salesforce reports, so it wants to be the name as Salesforce writes it. An agent who turns up in the queue but is not on this list still gets reviewed and still gets a coaching row — the list decides the ORDER of the sheet and who appears on it with no cases yet, not who may be reviewed.

How many cases at once

Each case is opened in a background window, read to the end of its feed and closed again. More at once is faster and heavier: every case being read is a full Salesforce page in memory. Two is comfortable on a support laptop; go higher only if the machine has room, and expect the reader window to surface more often.

The AI

Reviews are written through the Microsoft 365 Copilot relay: the tool drives a Copilot tab in a window of its own, the same way the SOTI AI Analyser does, so no case data goes anywhere the browser was not already signed in to. Chrome has to grant access once — press Grant access to Copilot and allow it when asked. It asks for m365.cloud.microsoft and copilot.cloud.microsoft together, because Microsoft now moves the chat from the first to the second; the connection is tested as soon as it is allowed.

On by default, and worth leaving on. Every name in a case — the agent's, the customer's, anyone who wrote on it — is replaced with a role label before the case is sent, and the labels are swapped back for the real names in the answer, so the review you read is unchanged. It is also what makes the review arrive at all: Copilot refuses outright to assess an identifiable employee, and a refusal comes back looking like an answer with every column empty. Turn it off only to compare.

The case goes over as a file. A chat box has a character limit and a case does not — a long email chain is hundreds of kilobytes, and it is exactly the case a review most needs to read in full. So only the instructions are typed into the box; the facts, the measured findings and the whole transcript are attached beside them as <case number>-case-material.txt, complete and untrimmed. Every case, however small — a support case is prose, and even a busy one with thirty messages on it is only about seven thousand characters, which is under the size the relay would otherwise judge worth uploading. The card on every review says how that case was sent.

—

Test the case upload sends a made-up case with three markers hidden at its start, its middle and its end, and asks Copilot to read all three back. Passing it proves the whole path — the file reached the site, the site read it, and nothing in the middle was dropped — which is the one thing a working relay test does not tell you. Worth pressing once after Grant, and again if a review ever comes back talking about a case you do not recognise.

Display size

Makes everything in the QA Tool smaller or larger — text, buttons and the case columns together — so more fits on a small screen. It changes straight away and is kept on this computer. Ctrl + and Ctrl − zoom from anywhere in the panel, and Ctrl 0 puts it back to 100%.

Your data

Every review, chat, coaching row and setting this tool keeps, written to one file — and read back in from one. Restoring adds to what is here rather than wiping it: the reviews, chats and settings in the file replace the ones now stored, and anything the file does not hold is left alone.
